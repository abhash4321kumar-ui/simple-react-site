Features:

Home Page: Ek welcome page.
Products Page: Jahan saare products ki list dikhegi.
Product Detail Page: Jab kisi product par click karenge, to uski detail page khulegi.
Add to Cart: User products ko ek shopping cart mein add kar payega.
Search/Filter: Products ko naam se search karna.
Navbar: Home, Products, aur Cart pages ke beech navigate karne ke liye.
